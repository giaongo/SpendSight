# My money

Open `index.html` in a browser. The list on the left jumps to a screen. The phone is the prototype. Nothing here is connected to S-Bank, and the coach does not call a model. Every figure comes from one sample ledger.

The customer is Aino Korhonen, Helsinki, one person, co-op member, S-Etukortti Visa. The ledger is October, rolled up by place, and every payment from 1 to 13 November. Today in the prototype is 13 Nov.

## What the phone does

Today leads with what is safe until Wednesday, then October’s food and drink, then the Bonus move. Spending lists every category. A row opens the places. A place opens a sheet you can drag down. Baskets are the three kinds of payment: must every month, must when it happens, and everyday. Inside everyday, groceries stay separate from the part that slips through.

This week shows the holds that have to stay in the account, then a choice between a calmer week and October’s pace. Dining, minus 20% is a slider. Bonus is the step table, a preview of the next shop on the green card, and the October lesson. Coach answers a fixed set of questions from the same ledger. The A in the corner says whose payments these are.

The back control and a drag from the left edge return along the path you came. Sheets follow the finger, take the release velocity, and settle. A flick closes. Reduced motion removes the travel and keeps the change.

## Bonus

The step applies to the whole month once the household reaches it. Under €50 the Bonus is €0. The steps used here are 1% at €50, 1.5% at €200, 2% at €300, 2.5% at €400, 3% at €500, 3.5% at €600, 4% at €700, 4.5% at €800, and 5% at €900.

November is on the 1.5% step. €214.00 × 1.5% is €3.21. The next step is 2% at €300. The gap is €86.00: a €70 grocery shop and €16 of café visits. €300.00 × 2% is €6.00. Those €86 were going to be spent anyway. The preview shows that.

If the €86 is new spending on top of the same shops elsewhere, Bonus rises €2.79 and the person is €83.21 poorer. The screen says so.

The food plan for the rest of November is €280, the same hold as on This week. With the café visits, S-group purchases reach €510, the 3% step, Bonus €15.30. Groceries alone would stop at €494, the 2.5% step, Bonus €12.35.

October closed at €652, the 3.5% step, Bonus €22.82, treated as paid on 10 Nov. €48 more at Prisma, taken from the €111 at K-Market and Lidl, would have made 4% and €28.00.

Paying the moved amount with S-Etukortti Visa adds the 0.5% payment benefit on that amount only. It is shown beside the step, not folded into it. Säästäjä is a confirmation, and the preview does not move money. October’s Bonus is already on S-Tili.
